# Records Custodian Deposition Excerpt

## Custody of the Binder

EXAMINING COUNSEL: Where was the original binder kept during March?
THE WITNESS: In the records room. I kept a working copy at my desk.
EXAMINING COUNSEL: So the original never left the records room?
THE WITNESS: It left once. I signed it out on March 3 and returned it on March 5.

## Review of the Copy

EXAMINING COUNSEL: Did anyone compare the copy against the original?
THE WITNESS: I compared all 42 pages before the binder went back.
DEFENDING COUNSEL: Objection to form.

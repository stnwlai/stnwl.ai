# Stonewall Showcase

Stonewall is a citation-first legal document intelligence platform. This section is the durable narrative edition of the showcase: the same product thesis presented in a format that can be read slowly and preserved without depending on a live interface.

Start with [Quarterback](quarterback/README.md). It gives the core product argument in one place, then branches into the official brief, the architecture, the workflow layer, and the publication runbook.

## What this section covers

- content-addressed evidence bundles
- line-verifiable citations
- explainable retrieval
- exact-tree publication checks

## Why it matters

Stonewall shows that useful legal document intelligence can keep retrieval and provenance in the same contract. The corpus is the substrate; citations and verification make that substrate operational.
